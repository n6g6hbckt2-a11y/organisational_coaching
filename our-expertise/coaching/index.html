Coaching & Supervision

We believe that coaching is a collaborative process of enabling individuals, groups or teams to experience themselves and their situation from a fresh perspective.

Together we make the difference.

Together we make the difference.

Executive Coaching

Is for any person in an organisation and not limited to their work role. We coach the whole person, because we believe that separating work and life misses the connection between both.

We appreciate that organisations can be everything from a source of joy and connection, whilst also being able to stimulate frustration and anxiety. Coaching enables individuals who are open to learning, to experience their situation from a fresh perspective.

We identify the objectives which matter for you and we engage your organisation to identify the objectives that matter for them. Together this makes the difference.

Over a period of time agreed upfront, we meet either face-to-face or virtually to consider the challenges and opportunities which are unique to you as a person seeking to learn, adapt and grow.

Team Coaching

Is for any Team within an Organisation and is not limited to the Team. We discover together what the unique characteristics are that make up the Team. What exists within your situation and what is being demanded from outside from stakeholders.

Most often, tensions exist between team members and between stakeholders. These are the connection points between people. Therefore, we coach these connections and situations to shift from draining tension to creative tension. From low-trust to high-trust.

Of course, Groups and Teams are made up of individual people. So our coaching is at the individual, Team and Organisational level at all times because a Team never exists in isolation.

Together we make the difference.

Organisational Coaching

Is for an Organisation seeking a variety of change or transformation and yet is experiencing a sense of being stuck in the beginning, middle or end of their existing situation!

Our objective analysis and enquiry will enable us to co-create what will make the difference for your Organisation. Unlike consultants, we do not arrive with the latest research and approach to “solve” your situation.

Rather, we take a relational approach to understanding what is really going on and to design unique interventions to support the Organisation to get to wherever it wants or needs to go.

This may then include 1:1, Team Coaching and Team-on-Team Coaching. Additionally, it can include development and training journeys to transform skillset as well as mindset if required.

Supervision

Those who master coaching recognise that we all have learning edges and benefit from professional support. We supervise external and in-house coaches and coaching leaders, for continuous learning and development, both 1:1 and in Groups.

Our supervision practice is deeply relational. This means that we recognise our connection and relationship with others is what shapes who we are and what we do.

This is a philosophy of being. That our sense of self is continuously emerging through being in relationship with individual others and also in the wider context.

During supervision, we explore from the perspectives of coach, coachee and supervisor and the relationship between them and their specific contexts. This is a dynamic learning environment for a coach or group of coaches.

We explore these dynamics drawing on an established process model of supervision and supported by a range of theoretical models.
