Our expertise

We have a passion for the work we do. Within this is a humility that coaching is not always the answer to an organisational challenge. With that in mind, we bring a range of interventions which fit within three areas: coaching and supervision, consultancy, and leadership training.

What makes an expert?

An expert by definition, is someone who brings expert skill or knowledge in a particular field. What we recognise in our field, is that if you ask 10 coaches how they coach, you are likely to get 10 different answers! We regularly ask ourselves what expertise actually is in coaching.

Our observation is that coaching mastery is associated with being comfortable with being uncomfortable. With being sufficiently humble to unlearn on a regular basis and to challenge orthodoxy and assumptions. This requires a deep curiosity of ourselves and relationships.

It helps that we have people with a broad range of experience working within organisations as expert contributors in different disciplines, as senior leaders, outside of business as social workers, counsellors or therapists.

Together, our range of experience and expertise makes the difference.
