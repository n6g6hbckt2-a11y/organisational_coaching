Consultancy

Strategic board advisory and practical people expertise to help your organisation thrive.

Non-Executive Directorship

Strong boards benefit from independent perspective, constructive challenge, and seasoned commercial judgment. Whether you are scaling up, navigating a critical transition, or strengthening your governance, an experienced Non-Executive Director brings clarity and steady counsel to the boardroom table.

Our network of seasoned executives and board advisors brings decades of leadership experience across diverse sectors—including deep expertise in guiding business transformation and helping organisations thoughtfully embrace AI. We get alongside founders, CEOs, and leadership teams to provide:

  • Independent strategic challenge and sound commercial oversight
  • Guidance on business transformation, innovation, and embracing AI responsibly
  • Board-level governance, risk awareness, and decision-making support
  • Experienced counsel on organisational culture, leadership succession, and people strategy
Bring seasoned, independent perspective to your boardroom.

Organisational Design & People Transformation

Does your organisation’s structure support your strategy, or are silos, inefficiencies, and cultural barriers holding you back? Do you have the right skills for today and the future?

Change is challenging – especially when people are involved. We get alongside and help you navigate it with:

  • A full review of your operating model
  • Implementation support, including costings, communication, and post-transition reviews
  • Expertise in redundancy programmes, M&As, and TUPE transfers
Let’s build a structure that drives success.

Culture & Engagement

Today’s workplaces face complex challenges—multi-generational teams, work-life balance, wellbeing, hybrid work, and DEI priorities all shape culture and engagement.

We can help simplify this by:

  • Designing engagement surveys and tools
  • Improving new starter and exit processes
  • Developing culture surveys and change roadmaps
  • Training managers in psychological safety and DEI awareness
Create a thriving, inclusive workplace with our support.

Performance Management & Development

Are your performance systems, processes, and policies driving success? Do employees understand what’s expected of them?

We are experts in:

Performance Management

  • Developing performance management policies and toolkits
  • Training and coaching managers to drive performance through feedback and clear expectations

Leadership Development

Strong leaders are key to success. We support leadership growth with:

  • Management and line manager development
  • High-performance and team development programs
  • Leadership profiling and bespoke training
  • Leadership and transitional coaching
  • Women in Leadership, Emerging Leaders & Graduate Programs
Empower your people to perform at their best.

Reward & Benefits

Pay and benefits are major costs – getting them right is crucial. While pay alone rarely motivates, unfair or outdated reward structures can harm engagement and performance.

Our experts will help by:

  • Developing reward strategies and pay policies
  • Managing salary and bonus review processes
  • Conducting benefits reviews and tailoring plans
  • Designing incentive schemes to drive business outcomes
  • Implementing total reward statements
Ensure fairness, motivation, and ROI with a well-designed reward strategy.

  • Generalist HR business partnering

HR Projects & Change Management

  • Policy creation and key process support
  • New starter inductions for retention
  • HR dashboards to track performance
  • Career progression tools and templates
Strategic HR support to drive business success.
