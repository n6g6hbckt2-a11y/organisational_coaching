Leader Diploma Programme

Our ICF-Accredited Coaching Education Level 1 programme consists of nine core days of training providing 65+ hours of coach training, 10 hours of mentor coaching and a final performance evaluation.

This programme meets the requirements for an ICF ACC Credential, excluding client coaching hours.

This highly experiential programme is delivered as a blend of face-to-face and virtual sessions, to reflect the reality of coaching today.

Our mission and vision as a ICF Coach Education Provider

Empowering futures through transformative coaching, our mission is to cultivate a community of ICF-certified coaching leaders who lead with empathy and expertise.

We are dedicated to providing dynamic education that fosters relational connections, enabling our learners to make a profound impact on individuals and organisations alike. Grounded in a commitment to excellence, we strive to be the catalyst for positive change through coaching.

Our vision is to be impactful through relational organisational coach education, shaping leaders to navigate complexities with curiosity and compassion.

Through our transformative programmes, we aspire to contribute to a global landscape where coaching becomes a cornerstone for personal and professional growth, fostering a culture of understanding, growth, and continuous curiosity.
