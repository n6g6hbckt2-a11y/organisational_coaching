Kerry

Hello, I’m Kerry Huggler and I support the team at Organisational Coaching behind the scenes and cover everything related to the back office.

Professionally, my past experience includes working in finance and administration in global organisations across countries and regions for 15+ years. My priorities refocused after starting a family and today I manage multiple interests much closer to home, drawing on my broad range of skills to streamline Organisational Coaching and volunteering my time and skills with not-for-profit organisations based in Zurich. I am an Australian Certified Practising Accountant (CPA) and a European Divisional Council Member of CPA Australia, which allows me to engage and support other CPA’s that are based in Europe.

Together with my husband and two sons, we have lived in Switzerland since 2014. I’m a struggling, but committed to learning, German speaker. When we have family time, in summer you will find us swimming in Lake Zurich and in winter you will find us in the mountains.
